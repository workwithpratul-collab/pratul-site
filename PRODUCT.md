# Product

Written from CLAUDE.md, content/site-copy.md and the premium redesign brief (no interview: the brief
asked for one uninterrupted run with no questions).

## What it is
Pratul's personal portfolio. Its homepage opens with "The Ascent": a real-time 3D film of a Himalayan
trek, driven by scrolling, from a night base camp to a sunrise summit. The summit is the hero; the page
below it is the services, work, process, story, tools and contact.

## Who it's for
Owners of small and mid-sized businesses (clinics, nurseries, agencies, shops), in India and abroad, who
want a website that makes their brand feel bigger than it is and helps visitors book, buy or call.
They arrive from a link (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram), mostly on phones, sometimes on a laptop in the
evening. They decide in under a minute whether Pratul is serious.

## Mode
Experience for the film and hero (the work is the interface), Persuade for the sections below
(the visitor decides to book a call).

## What they must leave with
1. "He builds things nobody else around me builds." (the film, the 3D moments)
2. "It would work for my business." (services in plain words, honest projects)
3. "I can reach him easily." (Book a call, a short form, WhatsApp)

## Brand commitments
- Honest: every project is labelled as a concept; no invented clients, stats or quotes.
- Calm and cinematic, never flashy. No sound effects.
- Painted, stylised, soft. Warm colour only from light sources.
- Fast: a premium site that stutters is not premium.
