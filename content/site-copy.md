## About (story)

I'm Pratul, a web designer and developer.

I fell in love with code because of what it can show. A few lines can become a view that stops you, like reaching the top of a trail and seeing the whole valley open up below.

That's how I see websites too. The climb is the hard part: understanding a business, its people, and what makes it different. The view at the top is what its customers get to feel.

When I'm not building, I'm on a trail, in the gym, on a football pitch, or lost in a book.

I work with businesses worldwide, from India to anywhere.

## Services

- **Cinematic websites:** scroll films and motion that make a brand feel big
- **Interactive experiences:** 3D product views, sliders and effects visitors can play with
- **Design that converts:** layouts that guide visitors to book, buy or call
- **Online stores and booking:** ordering, enquiry and WhatsApp flows
- **Found and fast:** SEO and speed, so customers find you and stay

## How I work

1. **Understand:** I learn your business, your customers, and what makes you different.
2. **Design the experience:** I plan how your visitors should feel and what they should do, including the interactions you want.
3. **Build:** I bring it to life, sharing progress so you can give feedback along the way.
4. **Launch and refine:** we go live, test on real devices, and polish.
